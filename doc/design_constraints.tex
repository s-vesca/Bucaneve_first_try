Design constraints for the sensor are reported in table \ref{TAB_CONSTRAINTS}.

\begin{table}[h]
    \centering
    \begin{tabular}{|c|c|c|}
        \hline
        Parameter & Value & Measurement Unit\\
        \hline
        $Vcc$ & $5:24$ & $V$\\
        $PPR$ & $64$ & $-$\\
        \hline
    \end{tabular}
    \caption{Sensor Design Constraints}
    \label{TAB_CONSTRAINTS}
\end{table}

The sensor should be a one to one replacement for a previous one, and so its 
dimensions are constrained.

The outline of stationary part is shown in figure \ref{FIG_MECH_DIM_PCB}, it has
an outer diameter of $64 mm$, and an inner one of $36 mm$ to let the motor shaft 
pass through it.

\begin{figure}[h]
    \centering
    \includegraphics[width=0.5\linewidth]{img/coil_board_shape.png}
    \caption{Mechanical drawing of the \ac{PCB}}
    \label{FIG_MECH_DIM_PCB}
\end{figure}

The target outer diameter is constrained by mounting requirements to $50 mm$, 
while the inner one has to be $27 mm$ to be mounted on the shaft.
